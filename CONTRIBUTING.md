# Contributing to CivilKit Calcs

Thank you for helping. Small, focused changes with a test are the easiest to review.

attachment or any other material) you agree that:

1. It is licensed to the project and to everyone who receives it under the same MIT licence.
2. The project maintainers may also include it in other software they publish or sell, whether open source, commercial or
   closed source, as the MIT licence allows. You keep the copyright in your contribution.
3. You wrote it, or you have the right to submit it under these terms. If your employer has rights in your work, you have
   their permission.

## Sign your commits

Add a `Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>` line to each commit (`git commit -s`). It states that you agree to the
[Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/) and to the terms above.

## What not to submit

* Code taken from software under a licence that is not compatible with MIT (for example GPL, AGPL or a licence that
  forbids commercial use), or from a proprietary product.
* Text, tables, figures or formulas copied from a published standard, code of practice or textbook, unless the owner has
  given written permission that you can show. Write results from the clauses in your own words and cite the clause
  number; do not reproduce the publication.
* Material from a worksheet or file that you do not have the right to share.
* Passwords, keys, personal data or customer data.
