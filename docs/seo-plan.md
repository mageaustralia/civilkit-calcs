# SEO plan for CivilKit Calcs

The goal is to be found by engineers searching for what the app does, from static pages that load fast, say plainly what the
tool does and does not do, and lead into the app. The pilot is ten pages under `/calcs/learn/<slug>/`, generated by
`tools/make_learn_pages.mjs` from a data table (`tools/learn/pages.mjs`) and a shared template (`tools/learn/render.mjs`).

## Search intents

Three kinds of query, each with a different page shape.

1. **Replacement searches** ("mathcad alternative", "calcpad online", "excel alternative for engineering calculations", "free
   engineering calculation software"). The reader compares tools. The page states what the tool does, where it differs and
   when the other tool is still the better choice. Naming other products is allowed on these marketing pages only.
2. **Task searches** ("simply supported beam calculator", "retaining wall stability calculator", "pad footing bearing
   pressure", "pipe flow darcy weisbach calculator", "pump operating point"). The reader wants a result now. The page gives the
   formulas in plain text, a worked example with numbers from a real engine run, the sheet source, a note that it is a generic
   textbook calculation and not a design-code check, and a button that opens the template (`/calcs/?sample=<id>`).
3. **How-to searches** ("convert mathcad worksheet", "import xmcd", "export calculation to word with equations", "units in
   calculations"). The reader has a job to finish. The page is a short procedure with its limits and the matching video.

## Indexing

* Site structure: the product page `https://civilkit.io/calc/` is the content root. Every landing page, the hub, the guide
  and What's new link "CivilKit Calcs" to it, and the app (`/calcs/`) only from "Open" buttons. Breadcrumbs (visible and
  BreadcrumbList) read CivilKit > Calcs > Calculators and guides (or User guide) > page. `web/e2e/seo-check.mjs` holds
  these rules.

* `public/sitemap.xml` lists the app, the hub (`/calcs/learn/`), the guide chapters at their extensionless addresses and the
  landing pages (absolute URLs). It is served at
  `/calcs/sitemap.xml`. `public/robots.txt` is served at `/calcs/robots.txt`, which crawlers ignore: they read only the
  host's root `/robots.txt`, which is owned elsewhere. Add the same `Sitemap:` line there, and submit the sitemap URL in
  Search Console.
* The service worker leaves `learn/`, the sitemap and robots.txt out of its precache, so a crawler or a first visit is
  served the page and never the app shell.
* Link to the ten pages from the app's own pages that crawlers can reach (the guide introduction, the app footer) once the
  pilot proves out.

## What to measure

Search Console (property for `civilkit.io`, filter on the `/calcs/learn/` prefix):

* Pages indexed, and "Crawled, currently not indexed" or "Duplicate" reasons per page.
* Impressions, clicks, CTR and average position per page and per query. Group queries by the three intents above.
* Queries that land on a page it was not written for (a sign that a new page is needed).
* Core Web Vitals for the pages (they are static and have no script, so any regression is in the template).

GA4 (already live through Cloudflare Zaraz):

* Sessions and engaged sessions by landing page, filtered to `/calcs/learn/`.
* The click from a landing page to the app: the "Open this template" and "Open CivilKit Calcs" links (use the page path of
  the next page view, `/calcs/` with `?sample=`, or add an event on those links in Zaraz).
* Video plays on the pages (Zaraz can track `play` events on `video`).
* Return visits to `/calcs/` from visitors whose first page was a landing page.

A page is working when it gets impressions for its target query within a few weeks, and a meaningful share of its sessions
reach the app. Rewrite pages that rank on page two and have a weak CTR (title and description first), and merge pages that
compete for one query.
